Motion presets. `animated` now takes a preset: `draw` (what `animated` alone means), `pop`, `bounce`, `ring`, `spin` and `pulse`, on the React, Vue and Svelte components and on every look. New `IconSwap` for React cross-fades between two icons, for toggles like menu and close. Loader, refresh, heart and bell join sun, moon and theme-system with ready-made `-animated.svg` files. Plain CSS inside the SVG, tree-shakeable, and it respects reduced motion.
