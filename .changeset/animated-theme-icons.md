Animated theme icons. New `theme-system` icon (373 icons now), an `animated` prop on the React, Vue and Svelte components that pops an icon in and draws its strokes, a `ThemeIcon` for React that shows sun, moon or a display and replays the motion when the theme changes, and `-animated.svg` files for sun, moon and theme-system. The motion is plain CSS inside the SVG and respects reduced motion.
